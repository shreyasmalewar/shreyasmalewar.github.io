##### Computer Scientist | AI Research & Medical Imaging

I am a Data Scientist and Instructor at <a href="https://www.newtonschool.co/" target="_blank">Newton School of Technology</a>, where I teach Introduction to AI/ML, Systems, and AI Essentials. I am also a Ph.D. student at <a href="https://manipal.edu/" target="_blank">Manipal Academy of Higher Education</a>, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RGzx9_4AAAAJ" target="_blank">Dr. Pawan Jogi</a>.

Previously, I worked in cancer research and earned my Master's in Computer Science from <a href="https://www.usc.edu/" target="_blank">USC</a>, where I conducted research on deep learning for medical image segmentation at the <a href="https://sites.usc.edu/uscradiomicslab/" target="_blank">Radiomics Lab</a>. My research interests include AI/ML, medical imaging, foundation models, MRI reconstruction, and neuroimaging.

Beyond research and teaching, I enjoy exploring diverse cuisines, staying fit, and reading about finance, science, and technology.
